## English short pitch
**Your sports schedule in one place.** Browse upcoming events, channels and potential streaming platforms in a mobile-friendly guide. Availability varies by location and provider. This app does not stream sports or include paid subscriptions.

## Potential audiences and validation
- Sports fans: confirm schedule usefulness, search and favorites in moderated user testing.
- Hospitality teams: validate whether schedule discovery improves guest information workflows; do not claim redistribution or public-performance rights.
- Partners: evaluate referral arrangements only after written commercial/legal authorization.
